# Rainy Night Konbini — Generation Prompt

Create one self-contained HTML file showing an interactive 3D miniature of a Japanese convenience-store street corner on a rainy night. Support mouse/touch rotation, panning, and zooming. Show no visible UI and no characters. Embed all required code and assets so the file works offline.

## Priorities

Prioritize physically plausible construction, clear pedestrian circulation, and a coherent composition. Preserve the anime atmosphere while using consistent real-world proportions. Include optional props only when they fit naturally; a shorter, well-designed scene is preferable to a crowded checklist.

## Layout

Use a complete square base approximately 14–15 scene units wide, with one unit representing roughly one metre. Keep every structure, prop, and cable within its boundaries. These dimensions guide the miniature's proportions and do not represent building-code requirements.

Make the convenience store, approximately 7 × 5 units, the visual focal point. An L-shaped street wraps the front and one side, clearly communicating a neighborhood corner.

Separate a shallow shop apron for outdoor fixtures from the public sidewalk. Maintain a continuous unobstructed walking route approximately 1.2–1.5 units wide. Connect it to the main entrance, side passage, and rear service door. Account for door movement, steps, fixture bases, and bicycle projections when measuring clearance.

Place one bicycle in a designated front-left bay, umbrellas beside the entrance, and recycling bins alongside the vending machines. Mount promotional posters on glass or walls to preserve walking space. Keep the rear simple: a service door and a rubbish bin, with a clear approach.

Use two or three short, grounded guardrail sections following the curb and connecting around the corner. Leave a generous opening aligned with the crossing and entrance, plus an open side passage.

## Physical construction

Every object must have visible, plausible support: ground contact, feet, a plinth, brackets, or a structural attachment. Avoid intersections between unrelated solid objects.

Model a recognizable city bicycle with two aligned wheels, a connected frame and fork, handlebars, saddle, pedals, chain, and a deployed stand. Both tires must touch the pavement.

Connect gutters, downpipes, equipment conduits, and utility wires to their actual endpoints. Support outdoor AC units on feet or brackets.

Signs must have solid backs and correctly oriented lettering. Keep street furniture near the curb, outside the clear walking route.

Use zebra-crossing bars whose long edges run parallel to the adjacent curb. Align the crossing with pedestrian access. Include parking markings only if a usable parking bay and vehicle access exist.

Traffic signals are optional. Include them only if the layout clearly establishes a controlled crossing or intersection. Their faces must point toward approaching vehicles, and their housings must attach to a supported pole or arm with plausible road clearance.

## Store interior

Through large glass windows, show a bright, warm, stocked interior: low shelves, beverage cabinets, bento and onigiri displays, snacks, a checkout counter, coffee machine, magazine rack, freezer, supported oden counter, promotional lightboxes, and a backroom door. Preserve connected aisles, cashier standing space, and access to both entrances.

## Art and lighting

Use clean toon shading, clear outlines, restrained materials, soft colors, and layered night lighting. Aim for a quiet, warm Japanese slice-of-life anime atmosphere and an exquisite collectible-model appearance.

Contrast warm interior light with cool wet streets. Place light sources inside their fixtures. Transparent glass must not cast opaque wall-like shadows. Keep shadows attached to their objects and prevent shadow acne or visible detachment. Wet reflections must follow the actual road and puddle surfaces.

## Animation

Add subtle continuous rain, eaves drips, expanding puddle ripples, glass streaks, gentle sign flicker, occasional sliding-door cycles, and softly changing wet reflections. Animate traffic signals only if included. Keep rain outside enclosed interior spaces.

## Build and review

Establish the base, building, circulation, and fixture footprints before adding detail. Then add props, lighting, and animation.

Before delivery, inspect the rendered scene from the front, rear, both sides, above, and close up. Check ground contact, mounting points, collisions, entrance clearance, connected walking routes, sign orientation, shadows, and reflection placement. Fix every observed inconsistency.

Verify mouse and touch interaction, animations, offline loading, and complete default framing on desktop and narrow screens. Use browser checks when available; state any checks that could not be performed.

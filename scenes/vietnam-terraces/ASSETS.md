## Rendering libraries

Three.js, OrbitControls and GLTFLoader r160.1, including the triangles utility needed by the loader, are embedded under the MIT notice in `index.html`. Source: [Three.js r160](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160).

Terrain, houses, thatch, rice, pasture, bamboo, brook, bridge, clouds and rain are generated by the scene code. Birds use the credited models above. No reference-photo pixels or unlicensed model downloads are included.

Terrace-bank color and relief textures are generated procedurally in the HTML. The low rim vegetation reuses the meadow blade geometry and material with smaller instance transforms. Reference photographs guide the distribution of earth and grass; their image data is not embedded in these textures.

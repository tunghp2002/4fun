# Nếp Núi — Vietnamese Rice Terraces

An offline, interactive square miniature inspired by northwestern Vietnam's golden rice terraces. Dense ripe fields continue across the crown; sloped green earth banks, a field shelter, two timber cottages with layered thatched roofs, a continuous brook and textured livestock complete the model. This is an imaginary landscape guided by the supplied photographs, not a reconstruction of a particular village.

Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser with WebGL. No server, installation, internet connection or visible controls are needed. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; on touch screens use one finger to orbit and two fingers to pan/pinch. `Home` restores the whole model, arrow keys orbit, +/- zoom and Space pauses motion.

Soft clouds drift above the model. A 72-second visual cycle moves through clear weather, gradual clouding, a shower and clearing. Rain ends at the terrain/canopy or a cottage roof; brook ripples accompany the rain. The cycle is decorative and does not simulate local climate. Reduced motion stops idle animation; hidden tabs pause rendering.

## Implementation and review

- [x] Terrain: nine solid, nested contour layers have horizontal planted surfaces and sloped earth banks. Shared color and relief textures show muted grass patches, exposed brown-gray soil and small grains instead of a uniform green face. Short grass fringes the rim only where vegetation is present, clear of access routes; bare soil appears on the square model cuts. All fields, including the crown, are planted densely. The brook runs from the right cut edge to the front cut edge, with a carved bed, translucent moving water and sloped banks; its water section stays visible at the model cuts. There is no hilltop tree grove or floating irrigation flume.
- [x] Rural details: cottage doors, windows, porch railings and layered thatched roofs follow the new reference. The field shelter is rotated to sit on a flat field; cottage pads avoid the hill's foot. Buffalo stand in the dry pasture; ducks float in the brook; textured hens stand in the yard clear of entrance steps and baskets. Doors and shutters pivot from their jambs; baskets sit on a widened veranda behind the balustrade. The bridge crosses the continuous brook on the path between the two cottages, with solid bank steps and clearance above the ducks. See [asset credits](ASSETS.md) for the buffalo, hen and mallard models and their adaptations.
- [x] Browser verification: the file loads offline with zero HTTP requests and browser errors. Assertions check dense coverage in every field, nine support samples on each pad, hen foot contact, hens clear of entrance zones/props, baskets clear of veranda rails, and ducks inside the brook. Seven regression checks verify outward horn faces, horn attachment, three-dimensional horn curvature, detailed textured birds, basket clearance, the two brook edge cuts and the support/collision audit. Five additional bank checks verify mixed soil/vegetation, bare cut faces, short grass with ray-measured root contact on the actual terrain, preserved crop/animal support and no browser errors. Desktop orbit/pan/zoom/Home, portrait framing, touch orbit/pinch, reduced motion, the visibility handler and multiple weather cycles were executed. Actual overview, close-up, reverse, overhead, phone and rain captures are saved below.

Browser checks ran with Chromium and software WebGL at 1600 × 1000 and 390 × 844 (pixel ratio capped at 1.6). Scene geometry and textures are shared; crop geometry and material quality stay fixed during movement. Rain/ripple meshes are allocated once and uploaded by the renderer on their first visible frame, then reused on later showers. Real-device GPU frame rate and background-tab behavior across other browsers were not measured. The buffalo body has no walking rig; its subtle breathing keeps hoof contact fixed, and the smaller companion uses shorter horns with a scaled body. The duck asset's rest pose is baked into a shared static mesh before scaling and cloning; the walking animation is not played while it floats. The original mesh detail and 1024-pixel bird color textures are preserved. This is a miniature landscape with adapted assets, not a species/breed reconstruction or a fluid simulation.

## Reference choices

The user's golden-field photo guides the full crown, close planted bands and small shelter; the later cottage photo guides timber facades, gables facing the porch and straw roofing. Tree placement follows this chosen composition, rather than a claim that all Vietnamese terraces lack trees. Ripe, drained fields were selected for the model, with visible water confined to the lower brook.

- [Vietnam Tourism: Mù Cang Chải](https://vietnam.travel/node/1524) describes the golden harvest season and its distinction from the water-pouring/planting season.
- [Vietnam Tourism: water-pouring and golden-season photographs](https://image.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/mu-cang-chai-spectacle-water-pouring-season) show green vegetated banks beside ripe golden rice. Bank vegetation is represented as patchy coverage over earth, rather than a green construction material.
- [IRRI: saving water through alternate wetting and drying](https://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/training/fact-sheets/water-management/saving-water-alternate-wetting-drying-awd) describes water levels dropping during grain filling and ripening.
- [Buffalo by hendrikReyneke](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/buffalo-14d376df3070448ba82e423c2392057b) provides the licensed textured body. The water-buffalo horn and forehead study is an adaptation, not an unchanged species identification of that source model.

Reference photographs are not embedded or redistributed. Preview images are captures of the working HTML.

## Repair review

The reported horn defect had inward-facing tube triangles as well as nearly planar curvature. The repaired horns have outward normals, thinner matte keratin and an upward/backward sweep; root contact was measured against the visible indexed head surface. The old chicken coordinates put birds on the entrance steps and inside basket footprints. Bird placement now uses the yard, supported verandas keep baskets behind the rails, and the layout audit covers the actual bird and prop bounds. The brook no longer terminates just behind the bridge; both ends pass through a model cut. Foundation samples caught two bank contacts during this change, which were corrected by adjusting the cottage pads. Browser checks and close captures were repeated after these corrections.

The terrace banks previously used one solid green material, obscuring the earth beneath their vegetation. Their new shared procedural textures retain green patches while exposing soil and subtle surface relief. The terrain and crop positions remain unchanged. A ray check caught grass roots placed above the polygonal slope by an analytic height estimate; the short blades now sit on the flat rim surface instead. Color and bump textures are generated once, and the rim blades reuse the existing meadow geometry/material.

## Aurora morphology

[NASA's archived Polar Aurora explanation](https://pwg.gsfc.nasa.gov/Education/waurora1.html) describes near-vertical rays forming changing arcs and curtains. [NASA Science: Auroras](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/auroras/) explains how atmospheric gases and altitude produce different colors. These inform the elongated ray structure, folding sheets and green-led palette.

The scene uses artist-designed shader motion, compressed atmospheric scale and decorative color blending. It is not a forecast or a physically calibrated model of an auroral event. No NASA photography or logos are included.

## Original geometry and textures

All terrain, shore rocks, snow caps, buildings, openings, window frames, interior furnishings, paths, stairs, pier, boat hull, wheelhouse, fittings, vegetation, smoke and auroral sheets are generated by the HTML. Wood grain, snow grain and smoke falloff are drawn locally in canvas. Stars are procedural geometry. All images in `images/` are captures of this runnable scene.

## Embedded rendering libraries

Three.js r160.1, OrbitControls and Reflector are reused from the repository's existing offline scenes. Their MIT notice is retained inside `index.html` and must remain with redistributed copies. [Upstream Three.js r160](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160).

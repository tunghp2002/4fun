# Art direction and asset credits

## Inspected illustration references

References were inspected on 2026-11-06. They inform palette, layered foliage silhouettes and the contrast between a quiet blue forest and small golden lights. The miniature is an original composition made with code; no reference-image pixels or source models are embedded or redistributed.

1. [Light in Darkness — Ddesignator, ART street](https://medibang.com/picture/o42009212244220530002699955/). A cool indigo/teal forest, a dark torii and scattered yellow fireflies. The visible signature identifies Ddesignator. The source page was discoverable but not readable through the page fetch tool; its full artwork was downloaded temporarily and visually inspected. No reuse license was established.
2. [Night Forest — Aesthetic Moon](https://aestheticmoon.bandcamp.com/album/night-forest). Illustrated cover with a layered blue forest, receding torii gates and small warm path lights. The cover image was visually inspected. Aesthetic Moon is the publishing account; the individual cover artist and image reuse license were not established. No music is included.

The temporary reference files are excluded from the scene folder. Photographs and real-world temple surveys were not used as visual references. Architectural proportions are deliberately compressed for a collectible miniature.

## Original procedural content

- Terrain, plinth, stream banks, bridge and railings.
- Curved shrine roof, timber frame, sliding doors, veranda, bell rope and offering fittings.
- Stone lanterns and purification shelter.
- Rooted trees, tapered branches, irregular overlapping crowns, individual leaf meshes, bamboo, ferns, grass and rocks.
- Painted wood-grain canvas texture, toon-light ramp and soft fog texture.
- Seeded firefly paths, independent fades, particle halos, current strokes and low mist.
- All preview images in `images/` are captures of the runnable HTML, not generated mockups.

## Embedded libraries

Three.js r160.1, OrbitControls and Reflector are reused from the repository's existing standalone scenes. They are distributed under the MIT license; the embedded library notices remain in `index.html`. See [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160) and its [license](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/LICENSE).

There are no runtime asset downloads, external fonts or third-party 3D model files.

# References and asset credits

Inspected on 2026-11-17. All geometry, textures and animation in this scene are original procedural work. Reference images were viewed temporarily and are excluded from the scene folder.

## Art direction

[Japanese House by the Water — Pinterest pin 703756186567143](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/japanese-house-by-the-water--703756186567143/) supplies the illustrated autumn palette, open timber tea-room atmosphere, pottery detail, overhanging maple foliage and calm reflective water. The artist and reuse license could not be established from the pin. No source pixels are reproduced, and the miniature uses its own layout and architecture.

## Koi anatomy

[Anatomy of a Koi Fish — Pinterest pin 281545414182787835](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/281545414182787835/) shows the shoulder/tail proportions, small eyes, sensory barbels, gill cover, dorsal fin, paired pectoral/pelvic fins, anal fin and caudal fin. The inspected illustration visibly carries the credit “Koi-Care.” Its original publication and reuse license were not established. The diagram is a construction reference, not a scene texture.

The original Next Day Koi anatomy page/image was also discovered but failed to load, so it was not used as the inspected image source. No photographic landscape references were used.

## Original scene content

- Closed parametric koi body meshes, thin ray-textured fins, eyes, gill lines, lips and barbels.
- Individually painted koi color maps, a shared scale bump map and fin membrane texture.
- Continuous body/fin deformation and independently scheduled random fish actions, with fixed-step replay for verification.
- Dry paved bench pockets with clear walking-corridor margins, randomized leaf descent parameters, nonperiodic drift and pooled intermittent surface rings.
- Pond outline, sand/stone bed, dry garden, plinth, paving, bridge and veranda foundations.
- Timber tea house, shoji, roof, tatami, cushions, table, ceramics, shelving and scroll painting.
- Curved tapering maple trunks/branches, serrated leaf silhouettes with veins and mottling, layered evergreen shrubs, connected fern geometry, flower textures, moss ground and weathered stone variants.
- Original canvas wood/bark grain, tiled roof color/relief, moss, stone, shoji paper, tatami weave and cushion fabric materials.
- Curved roof surfaces, ridge/eave tile caps, rafters, irregular beveled flagstones, copper rain chains, catch bowls, a stone basin and bamboo fencing.
- Falling/floating leaves, water ripple shader and underwater light approximation.
- Every image under `images/` is captured from the actual runnable HTML.

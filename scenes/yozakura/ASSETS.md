# References and asset credits

## Visual references

- Primary: [Night Cherry Blossoms at Hirosaki Castle — Pinterest pin 608689705908112658](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/608689705908112658/). The pin's preview image was opened and inspected. It shows two illuminated blossom banks reflected in a dark moat. This guides the arrangement, warm-pink illumination and calm water, rather than an exact site plan.
- Color study: [Cherry Blossoms by the Iwasakigawa River at Night — Pinterest pin 19632948348061135](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/19632948348061135/). Used to study cool blue water against pink blossoms and warm reflected light.
- Location context: [Hirosaki Park's night-sakura photographs](https://www.hirosakipark.jp/sakura/2017/04/8371/) show illuminated trees and reflections around the park's moats. This is a historical visual reference, not current visitor information or an event schedule.

The Pinterest images are reference-only. Their photograph/thumbnail pixels are not embedded, traced into textures or redistributed in this folder. The arched bridge, paths, benches and lantern arrangement are a compact garden design added for the miniature. No claim is made that they reproduce the exact spot, scale or camera location of the pin.

## Geometry and textures

The terrain, retaining walls, bridge, benches, lanterns, cables, tree roots, tapered branches, flower corollas, stalks and falling petals are generated by the scene code. Bark, gravel, soil, paper fibres, petal colors/stamens, soft lamp halos and the title plaque are generated locally in canvas. There are no downloaded 3D models, commercial texture assets, remote fonts or runtime asset requests.

## Rendering libraries

Three.js r160.1, OrbitControls and Reflector are reused from the repository's existing offline scenes. They are embedded in `index.html` with their MIT notice, which must remain with redistributed copies. Upstream: [Three.js r160](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160).

All preview images in `images/` are browser captures of this HTML.

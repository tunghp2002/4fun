# Art references and credits

## Inspected Pinterest artwork

- [Anime-Inspired Fireworks: Illuminate Your Artistry — pin 768145280220406157](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/768145280220406157/). The image was opened and inspected: an anime-style coastal bay, blue-violet sky, peach horizon and gold/pink/cyan fireworks with curved trails. Used for the palette, luminous shapes and festive atmosphere. The pin includes a promotional text panel; that panel is not reproduced.
- [Fireworks Anime — Colorful Night Sky Display — pin 676525175308363841](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/676525175308363841/). The image was opened and inspected: bright gold, pink and blue radial fireworks, glitter and trailing points. Used for burst shapes and luminous particle accents.
- [Anime scene of two people standing on a beach looking at the sky — pin 616571005275637540](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/616571005275637540/). The illustration was inspected as a secondary study of cool coastal sky colors and warm reflections. Its characters are not included.

These pins are artwork references. Artist identities and original licensing could not be established from the pin metadata. No reference-image pixels, characters, promotional text, watermarks, textures or downloaded models are redistributed in this scene. The composition is an original coded interpretation.

Photographic references were excluded after the user specified an art-based direction.

## Original code-generated assets

The terrain, beach, boardwalk, kiosks, fittings, tower, benches, cart, palms, grass, barges, sky texture, signs, smoke falloff and fireworks are generated locally by the HTML. Texture pixels are drawn in canvas; firework heads and trails are reusable geometry buffers. All images in `images/` are captures of this runnable HTML.

## Rendering libraries

Three.js r160.1, OrbitControls and Reflector are reused from the repository's existing offline scenes. Their MIT notice is preserved inside `index.html` and must remain with redistributed copies. [Upstream Three.js r160](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160).
